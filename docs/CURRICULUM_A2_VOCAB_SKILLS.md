## 1. Purpose

This document completes the A2 node-level curriculum design by adding:

- Vocabulary & lexical chunks (`VX`)
- Pronunciation (`PR`)
- Listening (`LS`)
- Speaking (`SP`)
- Reading (`RD`)
- Writing (`WR`)

These nodes extend the validated 124-node A1 graph and the 70 A2 Grammar/Communicative Function nodes in `CURRICULUM_A2_GRAMMAR_FUNCTIONS.md`.

The A2 boundary follows CEFR communicative outcomes: frequent language related to immediate relevance, simple routine exchanges, short social interaction, short clear messages/announcements, very short simple texts, and simple descriptions of background and immediate needs. The exact node inventory and order remain product-owned curriculum decisions.

# 9. A2 integrated exit profile

An A2 learner approaching completion should be able to:

- handle simple routine exchanges on familiar matters;
- sustain a short social interaction with basic follow-up/checking support;
- narrate a short past event and describe basic background;
- discuss plans/arrangements and routine appointments;
- compare familiar options and give a simple reason/recommendation;
- handle predictable shopping, restaurant, travel, health and service problems;
- understand the main point and key explicit details in short clear messages, announcements and conversations;
- follow short simple written texts and locate predictable practical information;
- write short notes, personal emails, simple narratives, requests and basic complaints;
- produce more connected, intelligible speech with improved endings, word stress, sentence stress, chunking and common contractions.

This is a CEFR-oriented curriculum exit profile, not an IELTS score equivalence.
