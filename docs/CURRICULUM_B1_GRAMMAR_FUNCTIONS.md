**Task:** P0-CUR-005A

**Date:** 2026-09-01  
**Status:** Detailed B1 grammar/function baseline

## 1. Purpose

This document extends the validated A1→A2 curriculum into B1 Grammar (`GR`) and Communicative Functions (`CF`).

The B1 proficiency boundary follows CEFR: understand the main points of clear standard input on familiar work/school/leisure matters, deal with most travel situations, produce simple connected text, describe experiences/events/hopes/plans, and briefly give reasons and explanations. B1 speaking should be connected and comprehensible even when pauses, planning and repair remain evident.

The exact grammar sequence is product-designed. CEFR describes abilities; it is not treated as a fixed grammar syllabus.

# 4. Recommended B1 waves

1. **Connected past and experience** — present-perfect system, past narrative, experience/detail.
2. **Opinion, reason and comparison** — concession, contrast, supported opinion, recommendation.
3. **Problem solving and practical independence** — work/travel/service problems, clarification, negotiation.
4. **Processes and information transfer** — passive, instructions, summaries, reported information.
5. **Possibility and hypothetical language** — modal possibility/deduction, second conditional, plans/predictions.
6. **Work/study interaction** — meetings, progress updates, phone calls, interviews.
7. **Longer connected production** — short presentations, stories, current-event discussion, unprepared familiar conversation.
