# B2 Vocabulary + Skill Node Inventory

**Task:** P0-CUR-006B  
**Version:** v1.0-draft  
**Date:** 2026-09-01  
**Status:** Detailed B2 vocabulary/skills baseline

## 1. Purpose

This document completes the B2 node-level curriculum design by adding Vocabulary (`VX`), Pronunciation (`PR`), Listening (`LS`), Speaking (`SP`), Reading (`RD`) and Writing (`WR`) to the B2 Grammar/Communicative Function layer.

B2 should support clear, detailed, increasingly spontaneous communication across familiar and moderately abstract topics, developed viewpoint/argument, extended listening/reading, professional interaction and the language operations that later support IELTS 6.5–7.0 preparation. It remains below C1-level density, precision and effortless flexibility.

# 9. B2 integrated exit profile

A learner approaching B2 completion should be able to:

- understand main ideas, supporting detail, argument, stance and moderate implication in extended clear-to-natural speech/text across familiar and moderately abstract topics;
- follow discussions with several speakers and identify positions, consensus and disagreement;
- communicate with substantially greater fluency/spontaneity while managing turns, clarification and repair;
- develop and defend viewpoints, handle counterarguments, qualify claims, discuss pros/cons and hypothetical consequences;
- participate actively in professional meetings, presentations, interviews, negotiation and problem resolution within familiar domains;
- summarize and begin to synthesize information from more than one source;
- explain trends/data, familiar technical processes and moderately complex cause/effect relationships;
- produce clear structured writing across opinion, report, proposal, formal correspondence, process, trend/data and evaluative genres;
- adapt register with greater control and use broader collocation, word formation, discourse markers and lexical precision;
- remain intelligible and increasingly natural through stress, prominence, connected speech, chunking, pausing and intonation rather than pursuing native-accent imitation.

This B2 curriculum is an appropriate foundation for serious IELTS 6.5–7.0 preparation, but internal B2 completion is not presented as an exact IELTS score.
